PHRASE FORMS THE PROGRAM ACCEPTS (write the sentence from these parts; every other wording is rejected)

Element nouns: wall, slab, space, door, window, column (or IfcWall, IfcSlab, IfcSpace, IfcDoor, IfcWindow, IfcColumn; "room" is accepted for a space in references). GlobalIds are 22 characters.
One element by identifier: "the wall with GlobalId '<id>'", "the wall with ID '<id>'", "the wall with the ID <id>", "the wall (ID: <id>)", "the wall with GUID <id>", "the wall identified as <id>", "the wall whose GlobalId is <id>", "the wall <id>", "wall ID <id>", "wall <id>".
One element by name: "the wall named '<name>'". A space may also be written "the space '<label>'".
A storey: "storey '<name>'", "the storey at elevation <e> m", or the identifier forms with the noun storey ("the storey with GlobalId '<id>'", "the storey identified as <id>", ...).
Where a new element goes (create): "on storey '<name>'", "on the storey with GlobalId '<id>'", "on the storey that contains the wall named '<x>'", "on the storey that the space named '<x>' belongs to".
Several elements by identifier: "the walls with GlobalIds '<a>', '<b>' and '<c>'" (also "with IDs", "with the IDs", "(IDs: ...)", "with GUIDs", "identified as", "whose GlobalIds are").
Sets: "all the doors on storey '<name>'", "every door hosted in the wall named '<x>'", "the walls that bound the space '<label>'", "only the walls on storey '<name>' longer than 3 m" (also "wider than", "taller than").
Targets by relation (R = an element by name/identifier, S = a storey, SP = a space reference):
  "the door on S that stands about 7.5 m north (+Y) of R"   (east +X, west -X, north +Y, south -Y, up +Z, down -Z)
  "the column furthest west (-X) on S"
  "the only slab contained in S"
  "the door hosted in R"
  "the wall that bounds both SP and SP" | "the wall that separates SP from SP"
  "the space bounded by R and R" | "the space bounded by R"
  "the wall connected to R"
  "the slab nearest to R on S"
  "the door on S that stands between R and R"
  "the slab on S directly above R" | "... directly below R"
  "the wall opposite R across SP"
  "the wall on the left-hand side as seen from R looking into SP" (left|right)
  "the second window from the west along R" (first..fifth)
  "the space on S that no window bounds" (window|door)
  "the wall on S that hosts no door and no window"
  "the wall bounding SP that hosts no window" (window|door)
  "the wall in which R is located" | "the wall that holds R" | "the wall R sits in"
  "the door that connects SP to the outside" | "the door that leads from SP to the outside"
  "the door that connects SP to SP" | "the door between SP and SP" | "the door joining SP and SP"
  "the wall that bounds SP" | "the wall bounding SP"
  "the slab below the walls of S" | "the slab the walls of S stand on"
  "the only wall on S that has no connection to another element" | "the wall on S with no connection to any other element"
  "the only space on S that has no space boundary" | "the space on S recorded with no space boundary"
Lengths: a number followed by m, metres, meters, mm or cm (write "0.4 m"). Points: "(x, y, z) m". Direction of a move: "1.2 m to the north (+Y)" or "by 1.2 m in the +y direction".
Optional closing constraint before the final period: ", keeping its placement fixed" | ", keeping its z value fixed" | ", preserving all of its relationships" | ", and make sure the other relationships stay consistent".
Verbs: Delete|Remove|Take out; Set|Change|Update; Move|Shift|Relocate; Add|Create|Insert; Copy|Duplicate; Rotate|Turn; Mirror|Reflect.
